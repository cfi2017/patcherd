patcherd has been installed.

1. Create a PatchRule to start patching binaries:

   apiVersion: patcher.k8s.io/v1alpha1
   kind: PatchRule
   metadata:
     name: my-patch
   spec:
     selector:
       matchLabels:
         app: my-app
     patches:
       - binaryPath: /usr/bin/my-app
         find: "DEADBEEFCAFEBABE"
         replace: "0001020304050607"

2. Verify the webhook is running:

   kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l {{ include "patcherd.selectorLabels" . | replace ": " "=" | replace "\n" "," }}

3. Check that injection works on new pods:

   kubectl get pod <pod-name> -o jsonpath='{.spec.initContainers[*].name}'
